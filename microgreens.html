AlfaAlfa

Alfalfa microgreens are delicate, nutty, and mild. They are a nutrient powerhouse, containing high concentrations of Vitamin K, copper, and folate. Their thin, crunchy stems make them an excellent addition to sandwiches and wraps for added texture and a boost of antioxidants.

Maintenance Store in a cool, dry part of the refrigerator. These are highly sensitive to excess moisture; avoid washing until ready to eat. Use a paper towel inside the container to absorb humidity and keep them crisp for 5-7 days.

Arugula

These microgreens pack a peppery, nutty punch that is much more intense than mature arugula. They are rich in glucosinolates and calcium. Their bold flavor profile makes them a favorite for topping pizzas, pastas, or steaks to cut through rich flavors.

Maintenance Store at 4°C in a sealed container. Arugula microgreens wilt quickly if exposed to dry air. Keep them in the crisper drawer and consume within 4 days for the best peppery bite and nutritional value.

₱45

Basil

Offering a concentrated sweet-savory aroma, Basil microgreens are a gourmet alternative to whole leaves. They contain high levels of polyphenols and Vitamin K. Perfect for garnishing caprese salads, pesto dishes, or infusing flavor into oils and dressings.

Maintenance Extremely sensitive to cold; do not place them at the very back of the fridge. Store at a slightly warmer refrigeration temp if possible. Avoid getting the leaves wet, as they can turn black if bruised or overly damp.

₱55

Broccoli

Broccoli microgreens are world-renowned for containing up to 40 times more sulforaphane—a powerful health-promoting compound—than the mature vegetable. They have a mild, fresh cabbage-like flavor that blends seamlessly into smoothies and salads.

Maintenance Highly durable compared to other microgreens. Store in an airtight container with a dry paper towel. They remain fresh for up to 10 days if the cold chain is maintained and moisture is managed.

₱50

Chervil

Known as "French Parsley," these microgreens have a delicate, lace-like appearance and a subtle flavor of anise or licorice. They are a classic "fine herb" used to enhance the flavor of poultry, seafood, and light cream sauces without overpowering them.

Maintenance Very fragile stems. Minimize handling to prevent crushing. Store in a rigid container with minimal airflow. Best used as a "finishing" herb—add to dishes at the very last second to preserve flavor and structure.

₱30

Cilantro

Cilantro microgreens offer a burst of citrusy, bright flavor in every tiny leaf. They are excellent for detoxification and are rich in Vitamins A, C, and K. Ideal for Mexican and Asian cuisines, providing a more refined texture than full-grown cilantro.

Maintenance These require consistent humidity. Store in a container with a slightly damp (not wet) towel. If they appear to wilt, a quick misting can revive them, but ensure they are consumed quickly after to avoid mold.

₱35

Red Amaranth

Highly prized for its stunning fuchsia-red color, Red Amaranth has a mild, earthy flavor similar to spinach. It is packed with betalain antioxidants and minerals. It is the go-to microgreen for chefs looking to add a dramatic visual contrast to any plate.

Maintenance Extremely delicate and high in water content. Avoid all direct contact with water during storage. Keep in an airtight container at 4°C. Best consumed within 3-5 days to maintain its vibrant pigment and crispness.

₱60

Red Beets

These microgreens feature a deep red stem and green leaves, offering a sweet, earthy taste just like the root vegetable. They are high in nitrates, which may support healthy blood pressure, and provide a beautiful vertical element to food plating.

Maintenance Durable stems but sensitive leaves. Store in a container that allows the stems to stay upright if possible. Keep refrigerated and dry. They can last up to a week if moisture levels are kept low.

₱55

Red Mustard

A spicy, wasabi-like microgreen with beautiful purple-tinted leaves. Red Mustard is high in Vitamins A and C. Its sinus-clearing heat makes it a perfect accompaniment for sushi, roast beef, or as a "kick" in a fresh summer salad.

Maintenance The spicy compounds are most potent when kept cold. Store in a sealed container. These are relatively hardy but will lose their "zing" if allowed to get warm or stay in the fridge for too long (over 7 days).

₱45

Dun Peas

Dun Pea shoots are crunchy, sweet, and taste exactly like fresh garden peas. They are high in protein and fiber. Their curly tendrils and robust stems make them a great base for salads or a nutritious garnish for stir-fries.

Maintenance One of the heartiest microgreens. They can be stored in a plastic bag with a bit of air. They hold up well in the fridge for up to 10-12 days. Can be eaten raw or very lightly sautéed.

₱35

Green Amaranth

A delicate, mild microgreen with a soft texture. Green Amaranth is a gluten-free source of nutrients, containing a full profile of essential amino acids. Its neutral flavor allows it to be added to almost any dish to increase nutritional density without changing the taste.

Maintenance Treat with extreme care as the stems are very thin. Store in a rigid container. Keep very dry; even a small amount of condensation can cause the leaves to clump and wilt. Consume within 5 days.

₱50

Mizuna

Mizuna microgreens have a mild, piquant, and slightly peppery flavor. Part of the mustard family, they are rich in antioxidants like kaempferol. Their feathery leaves provide a sophisticated look and a gentle "bite" to mixed green salads.

Maintenance Requires cold temperatures and high humidity. Use a container that seals well. If the leaves become limp, they can sometimes be revived with a 5-minute soak in ice water before being spun dry and served.

₱50

Radish Kaiware

Known as "Kaiware" in Japan, these radish microgreens have a significant spicy kick and a crunchy white stem. They are excellent for digestion and provide a concentrated source of Vitamin C. A traditional pairing for sashimi and hand-rolled sushi.

Maintenance Strong stems make them easier to handle. Store in a container with a paper towel. The spice levels remain highest when kept consistently cold. Can last 7-9 days in proper refrigeration.

₱40

Purple Kohlrabi

Featuring beautiful purple stems and green leaves, Kohlrabi microgreens have a mild, sweet flavor similar to a mix between cabbage and broccoli. They are exceptionally high in Vitamin C and fiber, perfect for adding color to coleslaws or sandwiches.

Maintenance Reliably hardy. Store in an airtight container. The purple color in the stems is stable, but the leaves should be kept dry to prevent yellowing. Good shelf life of up to 8-10 days.

₱40

Purple Radish

A visually stunning radish variety with dark purple leaves and stems. It carries the same spicy, peppery flavor as the Kaiware but with significantly higher levels of anthocyanins. Use it to provide both heat and a deep violet pop to your culinary creations.

Maintenance The purple pigment is water-soluble; avoid washing until the last second to prevent the color from bleeding onto other ingredients. Store dry in a sealed container for 7-9 days.
