Privacy Policy

At Nama Microgreens, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy outlines how we handle the data you provide while using our services.

1. Information We Collect

In order to provide our fresh urban crops to you, we may collect the following:

  • Personal details: Name, email address, and phone number.
  • Logistics info: Delivery and billing addresses.
  • Transaction info: Order history and purchase details.
  • Technical data: Website usage data (cookies, browser info).

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data allows us to maintain the quality of our farm-to-table service:

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver your orders accurately.
  • To communicate with you regarding your orders or inquiries.
  • To improve our website performance and overall customer experience.
  • To send optional updates or promotions regarding our harvests.

3. Sharing of Information

We respect your data. We do NOT sell your personal data to third parties. We only share information with trusted partners necessary for our operations:

  • Delivery Services: For shipping and logistics.
  • Payment Providers: For secure transaction processing.

4. Data Protection

We take reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational steps to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or misuse. Our systems are designed to keep your personal and transaction history secure.

5. YOUR RIGHTS

We believe you should have control over your data. Regarding your personal information, you may:

  • Request access: Obtain a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Request corrections: Ask us to update or fix any inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion: Ask us to remove your data from our active systems.
  • Opt-out: Unsubscribe from marketing messages or promotional updates at any time.

6. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our harvests or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted directly on this page, and we encourage you to review it periodically.
