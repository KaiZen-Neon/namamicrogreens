Gotu Kola

A staple of Ayurvedic medicine, this "herb of longevity" has a mild, mineral-rich taste with a slightly bitter finish. High in Vitamin C and Calcium.

Maintenance Store in a shallow, unsealed container in the fridge. For maximum potency, consume within 5-7 days. Avoid direct contact with freezer walls.

Pansit-Pansitan

Known as *Peperomia pellucida*, these succulent leaves offer a crisp, cool texture similar to celery. Traditionally used for its anti-inflammatory benefits.

Maintenance Sensitive to heat. Wrap loosely in a damp paper towel and refrigerate. Do not wash until right before you eat to prevent wilting.

₱50

Red Veined Sorrel

Prized by chefs for its striking red veins and sharp, citrusy tang. Contains high levels of Vitamin A and oxalic acid, which provides its signature tartness.

Maintenance Very delicate leaves. Store in an airtight container with a dry paper towel to manage moisture. Best kept at a consistent 4°C (39°F).
